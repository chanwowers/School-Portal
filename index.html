About the school

Established in 1986 as an extension of Las Piñas National High School, the school was created to accommodate the increasing number of students in the area. In its early years, classes were conducted in the evening due to limited classroom facilities. Over time, the school expanded with the construction of additional buildings and an increase in student enrollment. In 2007, it became an independent institution through Republic Act No. 9452 and was officially named CAA National High School.

The school was founded to bring quality secondary education closer to home for families in the area, and has since grown into one of the city's larger public high schools. Its academic programs are complemented by co-curricular activities in sports, the arts, and student leadership.

This portal is the school's online window for learners and parents: grades, schedules, enrollment, and official announcements, available whenever you need them.

Mission & Vision

The direction that guides everything we do for our learners.

Our Mission

To provide quality, equitable, and learner-centered basic education for every student.

We commit to a safe and supportive environment where learners are challenged to think critically, act with integrity, and serve their community — guided by capable teachers and supported by engaged parents and stakeholders.

Our Vision

A community of lifelong learners and principled leaders.

We envision graduates of CAA National High School who are equipped with the knowledge, skills, and values to succeed in higher education, the world of work, and service to the nation.
